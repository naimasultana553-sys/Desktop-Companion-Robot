Desktop Companion Robot

AI Mini Bot — Your Interactive Desk Companion

What is this?

An ESP32-powered desktop companion robot with an OLED face, pan/tilt head movement, and AI voice control. This interactive 3D explorer lets you learn every part, every wire, and watch the robot move — before buying any hardware.

Key Features

  • 🧩 Interactive 3D workbench with 17 color-coded wires
  • 🤖 Assembled robot preview with animated OLED face
  • 🩻 X-ray mode to see internal components
  • 🔌 Click any wire to trace its circuit net
  • ⚙️ Simulated pan/tilt servo control
  • 🖱️ Detach & reassemble parts interactively

Golden Rules

⚠️ Common Ground: All GNDs must join together

⚠️ Servo Power: Feed from external 5V rail, never XIAO's 3V3

⚠️ OE Pin: If PCA9685 has OE, tie it to GND

PROCESS

Build Process

From idea to final robot

💡 Idea

Conceptualize a desktop companion robot with AI capabilities

📐 Design

Circuit design, pin mapping, component selection, and 3D modeling

🖨️ 3D Model

Design the enclosure body, head shell, and neck arm in CAD

🔌 Circuit Design

Plan wiring on breadboard, I2C bus, and power distribution

🔩 Assembly

Build breadboard circuit, mount servos, wire PCA9685, connect OLED

💻 Firmware

Flash XIAO ESP32-S3 with Arduino code (face, servo, WiFi)

✅ Testing

Verify each subsystem: servos, display, power, WiFi, integration

🤖 Final Robot

Assemble in 3D-printed case, calibrate servos, deploy AI brain
